Spatial GUI is based on the behavior and configuration concepts of
https://github.com/tastytrash/Spatial-GUI by tastytrash and its contributors,
reference commit 88eeb54a262eff78ac2857995d8d9d1dd533dd22.
The upstream MIT license is reproduced verbatim in LICENSE and packaged at
mixins/spatial_gui/LICENSE in the native archive.

Horizon (https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon) and horizon-mixins
(https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon-mixins) are MIT-licensed build/runtime
dependencies. Their source and binaries are not included in the .hnmod.

This bundle contains no Mojang game code or assets. Prepared resources must
come from the player's own Minecraft installation through Horizon.
